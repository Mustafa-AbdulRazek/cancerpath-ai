# 2-minute video: simple script (hard limit 2:00)

Record the **app** (https://cancerpath-ai.vercel.app), not the slides. About 200 words. Speak slowly; short pauses are fine.

## Before you record (5 minutes)
1. Open Chrome with only one tab: https://cancerpath-ai.vercel.app
2. Press **F11** (full screen). Zoom to 110% (Ctrl and +).
3. Open https://cancerpath-ai.vercel.app/county/09 once, click **Suomi**, then **Write brief with Gemini**, and wait until it finishes. Now it is cached and will be fast in the video. Go back to the home page.
4. Close notifications, Teams, WhatsApp and anything else that can pop up.

## Script

| Time | What you show | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home page, top | "In Finland, one in three cancer patients needed three or more visits before diagnosis. In some regions, cancer survival is going down. But nobody can see where the time is lost." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Move the mouse over the map, then the list "Where to look first" | "This is CancerPath AI. For each of the 22 regions, it shows where the cancer pathway falls behind, and what to do about it. It uses only public data." |
| 0:30–0:50 | Click **Päijät-Häme** in the list. Point at the four boxes. | "Let's open Päijät-Häme. Here, colon cancer survival fell from 70 to 63 percent. Finding cancer early looks normal, so the problem may come later: after the first doctor visit. Today, nobody measures that step." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Point at the blue box **Do this first**. Click **Show plan** on one action. | "So the first action is: measure the time from referral to treatment. Every action has an owner, a target, the first steps, and the evidence behind it." |
| 1:10–1:25 | Scroll to **Looking ahead to 2035** | "We also look ahead. Will the region reach the 2035 targets? And how much more care will an older population need?" |
| 1:25–1:45 | Click **Suomi**, then **Write brief with Gemini**. Then click **Download 1-page brief**. | "The AI writes a short report for the region's leaders, in Finnish or English. Every number is checked against its source. They can also download a one-page brief." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Back to the home page. Scroll to **Proposed next step** | "Our next step is a 90-day pilot with one region. CancerPath AI: from where the gap is, to what to do on Monday." |

## Words that are easy to say wrong
- **Päijät-Häme**: "PIE-yat HA-meh"
- **Gemini**: "JEM-in-eye"
- Say **"region"**, not "wellbeing services county". It is simpler and correct for the video.

## How to record (free, already on Windows 11)
**Option A: Clipchamp (recommended, free, built into Windows 11)**
1. Open **Clipchamp**, then Create a new video, then **Record & create**, then **Screen**.
2. Pick the Chrome window. Record the screen **without talking** first, slowly, following the "What you show" column.
3. Then click **Record & create**, then **Audio**, and read the script while you watch your screen video. Drag the audio under the video.
4. Cut pauses with the scissors tool. Check that the total length is **under 2:00** (it shows at the bottom).
5. Optional: Captions, then turn on auto captions (helps judges).
6. **Export at 1080p.**

**Option B: AI voice (if you prefer not to speak)**
In Clipchamp, use **Text to speech** and paste the "What you say" lines. Choose an English voice and speed 0.9 to 1.0. Your own voice is more personal, but an AI voice is allowed.

**Option C: Xbox Game Bar (fastest)**
Press **Win + Alt + R** in Chrome to start and stop. It records voice and screen together, so you must talk while you click.
