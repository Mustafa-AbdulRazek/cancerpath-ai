Detection · wellbeing services county

HPV self-sampling kits for women still not screened after the reminder letter

  • Owner: county screening coordinator · KPI: participation ≥ 80%
  • Reach: ≈ 5,000 more women screened per round in Päijät-Häme
  • Learn from: South Savo, cervical screening 82.3%
  • Evidence: Finnish trials; letter first, kit as second reminder (BMC Women's Health 2015)
  • Roadmap: 2.3, 2.4

Nationally (STM, THL, FICAN):

  • A mandatory suspected-cancer flag in the national eReferral (2.5.2)
  • Publish referral-to-treatment times by county and cancer type (3.1.2, 3.1.4)
  • Stage recording as a quality indicator, target 70% (3.1.4, 3.2)

Built so a decision-maker can trust it, and a judge can check it

Sourced

A source link on every number. Reproducible pipeline from the THL and Statistics Finland APIs. Strategy facts cited by page.

Honest

Cancer mortality age-standardised by county (SMR, 95% CI), not crude rates. Incomplete HUS data excluded and hatched. GP waiting averaged over 3 years. Small survival differences not scored. Correlations checked without Uusimaa.

Guarded AI

Gemini sees only cited facts. Each number is checked against the source it cites. Briefs are cached, and there is a rule-based fallback.

Public aggregate data only · no patient data · MIT licence · Next.js, d3-geo, Google Gemini API
