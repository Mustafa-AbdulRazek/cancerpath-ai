CancerPath AI

Conversation guide for the AstraZeneca mentor · about 10 minutes · Team TachyKnights

Before you go: open cancerpath-ai.vercel.app on your laptop or phone. Have this page and a pen ready.

Part 1 · Say the idea first (1 minute)

"Hi, we're TachyKnights. Thank you for your time."

"Finland's cancer strategy says everyone should get equally good cancer care, wherever they live. But today no county can easily see where in the cancer journey its patients lose time: finding the cancer early, getting referred, or starting treatment."

"So we built CancerPath AI. For each of the 22 wellbeing services areas, it shows the step where that county falls behind, and it gives a 90-day plan: what to do first, who owns it, how to measure it, and the evidence behind it."

"It only uses public data from THL, the Cancer Registry and Statistics Finland. Every number has a source."

"Can I show you quickly? Then we have a few questions, because we want our ideas to be realistic."

Part 2 · Show the app, step by step (5–6 minutes)

  1. Home page: point at the map.
    "Each colour is the step where that county is furthest behind. The striped areas have incomplete data, and we say that openly."
  2. Point at the list "Where to look first".
    "This ranks the counties by their biggest gap. North Karelia is first: its specialist queues are the longest in Finland."
  3. Scroll to "Three things the data shows". Read finding 1.
    "Colon cancer survival differs by 11 points between counties. In Päijät-Häme it fell from 70 to 63 percent, while Finland improved."
  4. Click Open Päijät-Häme.
    "This is the county page. At the top, one sentence tells you where the gap is."
  5. Point at the four boxes in a row (the pathway).
    "Detection and referral look normal here, but the outcome is behind. So the problem may be after the first doctor visit. That step, diagnosis to treatment, is not measured publicly anywhere."
  6. Point at the blue box "Do this first".
    "Our first recommendation: start measuring time from referral to treatment, with the county's own data and THL's definitions."
  7. In "90-day plan", click Show plan on one action.
    "Every action has an owner, a target to measure, the first three steps, how many people it reaches, a county to learn from, and the action number in the national roadmap."
  8. Scroll to "Looking ahead to 2035".
    "Here we look forward: at the current pace, will the county reach the 80% screening target by 2035? And how much will ageing increase demand?"
  9. Click Suomi, then Write brief with Gemini. Wait a few seconds.
    "The AI writes a short brief for the county board, in Finnish or English. Every sentence cites its source, and the app checks every number against that source."
  10. Click Download 1-page brief (top right).
    "And this is a one-page version a director can print or email. It works even without the AI."
  11. Optional: go back and open North Karelia from the list.
    "Different county, different gap: here the problem is the referral step, so the plan starts differently."

If something is slow, use the "Download 1-page brief" button. It's instant.

Part 3 · Ask the questions (3–4 minutes)

Write down short answers. If she disagrees with something, just say "Thank you, that's really useful" and note it. We'll fix it.

1. "To show referral delays, we use the number of people waiting more than 6 months for any specialist care. It's not cancer-specific. Is that a fair signal, or do suspected cancer patients usually get a separate fast track?"
2. "Looking at our plan, which action would a county really pay for first? And is any of them unrealistic?"
3. "We'd like to suggest a 90-day pilot with one county. Which county would be realistic, and who usually makes that decision there?"
4. "Pirkanmaa records the cancer stage for 72% of patients, but the national level is 42%. Do you know why Pirkanmaa is so much better? Could other counties copy it?"
5. "In Päijät-Häme, colon cancer survival dropped from 70% to 63% in five years. Have you heard about this? Is there an explanation we should mention?"
6. "How does the AstraZeneca judging work on Sunday? Do you visit our table between 10:30 and 14:00, or is there a separate session at 14:00? How much time do we get?"
7. "If AstraZeneca or a county wanted to use a tool like this after Junction, what's the one thing it's missing?"
8. "Could we quote one sentence from you in our submission, as 'AstraZeneca Medical Affairs, mentor interview'? We can leave your name out if you prefer."

To close

"Thank you so much, this really helps. We'll update the tool with your input today."
